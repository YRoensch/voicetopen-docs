# 9. Contact & Support

If something isn't working, please check [Troubleshooting & FAQ](08-troubleshooting.md) first — most problems are covered there.

## Get in touch

Email: **peal-tables.3v@icloud.com**

To help us help you, please include:

- Your **macOS version** ( → **About This Mac**)
- Your **VoiceToPen version** (**VoiceToPen → About VoiceToPen**)
- What you were doing, and what happened
- The **exact error message**, if there was one (a screenshot is ideal)

> 🔒 **Never send us your API keys**, and remove any private content from screenshots. We will never ask for your keys.

## Problems with your ElevenLabs or Anthropic account

Billing, credits, usage limits and key management are handled by those services. Please contact them directly:

- ElevenLabs: [help.elevenlabs.io](https://help.elevenlabs.io)
- Anthropic: [support.anthropic.com](https://support.anthropic.com)

## Purchases and refunds

Purchases are handled by Apple. To request a refund, go to [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).
